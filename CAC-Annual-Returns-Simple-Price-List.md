# CAC Annual Returns — Simple Price List

**4 August 2026**

## The one thing to understand first

**The penalty is NOT one-off. You pay it for every single year you did not file.**

Miss 1 year, you pay 1 penalty.
Miss 4 years, you pay 4 penalties.

Each missed year has its own filing fee **and** its own penalty. That is why the amount
keeps growing. Every year you wait, it gets bigger.

## How you pay

**Step 1 — CAC money, paid first.**
This goes straight to government. We send you the Remita receipt so you can see it.
This money cannot be refunded once CAC has it.

**Step 2 — A2F fee, split in two.**
Half when we start. Half when your company shows **Active** on the CAC website.

You check it yourself. Go to **search.cac.gov.ng**, type your name, and look at the
status. When it says Active, you pay the second half.

We only get our full fee when you can see the result.

---

## Why fix this now

- Your company shows **"Inactive"** on the CAC website today. Anybody can check it —
  a bank, a customer, a partner.
- **Banks freeze company accounts** when the status is Inactive.
- You cannot get a loan or a grant with an Inactive company. No financier will touch it.
- If you leave it for 10 years, CAC removes your company from the register completely.
  Then you cannot trade, sign contracts, or use your bank account at all.
- The bill grows every single year. Today is the cheapest it will ever be.
