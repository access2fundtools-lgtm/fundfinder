# WhatsApp Funnel + Auto Replies

**4 August 2026 · for WhatsApp Business app on 08062085464**

---

## First, what the app can and cannot do

| Tool | Is it automatic? | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Greeting message | **Yes** | Sends by itself to anyone who messages you the first time |
| Away message | **Yes** | Sends by itself outside your working hours |
| Quick replies | No | You type `/word` and your saved message appears |
| Labels | No | You tag each chat by hand to track the stage |

**There is no chatbot in the app.** Only two messages send by themselves. Everything
else is you typing a shortcut. A real chatbot needs the API, which we parked.

That is fine. With four to ten leads a week, shortcuts are faster than a bot anyway.

---

## The funnel — 6 steps

| Step | What happens | Who does it | Label to set |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | They message you | They do | New lead |
| 2 | Greeting sends automatically | App does | — |
| 3 | They send name + type | They do | Checking |
| 4 | You check portal, send price | You (`/quote`) | Quote sent |
| 5 | They pay CAC money, you file | You (`/pay`) | Filed |
| 6 | Status is Active, collect balance | You (`/active`) | Done |

Then one more step: offer funding. That turns a ₦25,000 client into a FundFinder user.

# 1. GREETING MESSAGE — set this now

**Where:** WhatsApp Business → Settings → Business tools → Greeting message
**Set to:** Send to everyone

Copy this:

```
Welcome to A2F Partners.

We fix CAC problems — annual returns, registration, and getting your company back to Active.

To give you an exact price, please send:

1. Your company name or RC number
2. Is it an Enterprise or a Limited Company?

We reply Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm.
```

**Why it is written this way:** it asks for the two things you need before you can quote.
Most people answer straight away. That means your first real reply is already the price,
not another question.

---

# 2. AWAY MESSAGE — set this now

**Where:** Settings → Business tools → Away message
**Schedule:** Outside 9am–6pm, Mon–Fri

```
Thank you for your message. We are closed now.

We open Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm.

If this is about CAC annual returns, send your company name or RC number now. We will check the portal and reply first thing in the morning.
```

# 4. LABELS — create these seven

Tap a chat → three dots → Label chat.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| New lead | Just messaged, no details yet |
| Checking | They sent details, you are checking the portal |
| Quote sent | Price sent, waiting for them to decide |
| Paid | Money received, work starting |
| Filed | Submitted to CAC, waiting for approval |
| Balance due | Status is Active, waiting for final payment |
| Funding lead | Job done, now a FundFinder prospect |

Every morning, open the app and look at **Quote sent** and **Balance due**. Those two
labels are where your money is sitting.

---

# 5. When to chase, when to stop

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| No reply to greeting after 2 days | Send one short message |
| No reply after that | Stop. Label it and move on |
| Quote sent, silent 3 days | One message: "Still interested? No problem if the timing is wrong." |
| Quote sent, silent 7 days | Stop chasing |
| Balance due, silent 3 days | Send the search.cac.gov.ng link again. Seeing Active usually does it |

Never send a third chase. It does not work and it gets business numbers reported.
