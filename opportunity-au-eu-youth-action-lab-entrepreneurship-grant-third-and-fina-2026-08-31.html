About This Opportunity

Third and final call of the AU-EU Youth Action Lab Entrepreneurship Grants. Eleven youth-led non-profits will each receive €31,500 for a 12-month project running January to December 2027. Each round has a different focus; this last one funds advocacy that tackles the barriers young entrepreneurs face and shifts the environment they operate in. Grantees also get mentorship, capacity building and a peer platform across Africa and Europe. Deadline 23 September 2026 at 13:00 CAT, which is 12 noon Nigerian time. Information webinar 3 September 2026, 13:00 CAT (optional). Questions: entrepreneurship@aueuyouth.com
