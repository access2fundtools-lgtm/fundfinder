Organiser Outreach Protocol

Every organiser currently live on fundfinder.ng, the verified way to reach them, and the sequence. Contacts were read off live pages on 26 Jul 2026 — nothing here is a guessed address.

What we're actually working with

The offer — one sentence, used in every message

We already list your programme, and we route pre-screened, eligibility-matched applicants to it — free.
This is not a cold pitch and must never be written as one. We are already doing the thing; the message reports it and offers to do more of it. That is what earns a reply from an institution that ignores vendors. The deployment ask (an agent configured to their eligibility rules, feeding only qualifying applicants) comes second, after they have accepted free value.

Priority order — pitch top to bottom, not all at once

The cadence — 5 touches over 12 days, then stop

Start date Dates below recalculate. Weekend sends shift to the next weekday automatically.

Templates

Rules that keep this from backfiring. Send from the dedicated outreach domain only — never fundfinder@a2fpartners.com, whose reputation now carries your signup confirmations. Warm the new domain for ~2 weeks at low volume before touch 1. Seven organisers is not a campaign; send them by hand, spaced out, never as a batch.
WhatsApp is used once. Only AM-Steve House publishes a Nigerian mobile and reads as a peer operation. Cold-messaging the other six on WhatsApp would breach Meta's opt-in rule and put the pending new_funding_alert template and the whole WABA at risk before it launches.
X/Twitter is amplification, never a pitch. Touch 3 posts their open call to your audience and tags them. It is a genuine favour that creates a legitimate reason for touch 4 — do not DM a pitch on X.
