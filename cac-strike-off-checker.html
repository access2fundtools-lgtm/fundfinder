What this notice means

  • Legal basis: Section 692(3) and (4), CAMA 2020 — CAC may remove a company from the register where it believes the company is no longer carrying on business.
  • Why these companies: ten consecutive years without filing annual returns.
  • The window: 90 days from the 15 July 2026 notice — approximately 13 October 2026.
  • What fixes it: file all outstanding annual returns, update your records including Persons with Significant Control (beneficial ownership), then send evidence of compliance to struckoffcompanies@cac.gov.ng.
  • If you do nothing: the company's name is struck off. It stops being able to open or operate accounts, sign enforceable contracts, bid for work, or access funding — and restoring it later is slower and costlier than filing now.
  • Not on the list? That's this batch only (Batch 6). If your company has unfiled returns it can appear in a later batch — filing now keeps you clear.
Data source: Corporate Affairs Commission public notice, 15 July 2026 (Batch 6 — 100,000 companies).
