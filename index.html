Funding questions, answered

What Nigerian founders ask us most.

How do I find grants for my small business in Nigeria?

Start with programs that are actually open. FundFinder checks government agencies (SMEDAN, LSETF, BOI), foundations and accelerators every morning and lists only opportunities Nigerian businesses can apply to right now — with the official application link for each. Browse free on the Opportunity Hub.

Is FundFinder free to use?

Yes. Discovering opportunities and getting daily alerts is free. You only pay if you want AI help preparing an application. We never take a commission on any grant you win.

Do I need CAC registration to qualify for funding?

Many grants and nearly all loans require an active CAC registration — and thousands of companies are in the CAC strike-off pool without knowing. Check yours free with the CAC strike-off checker.

What government funding is open right now?

It changes weekly — that's the problem FundFinder solves. Federal and state programs like the SMEDAN Conditional Grant Scheme and LSETF MSME Loans open and close on their own schedules. The hub is updated every morning so you never apply to a closed program.

How does the AI matching work?

You describe your business once — sector, stage, state, registration status. FundFinder compares that profile against every open opportunity and alerts you only when you actually qualify, so you stop wasting time on programs that will reject you on page one.
