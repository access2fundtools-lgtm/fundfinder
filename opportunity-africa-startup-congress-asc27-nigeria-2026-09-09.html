About This Opportunity

The Africa Startup Congress 2027 is a continental gathering of founders, investors and policymakers at the Kigali Convention Centre from 24 to 26 February 2027, with more than 2,000 delegates expected. The theme is "Empowering Innovators, Accelerating Job Creation Across Africa".

The route in for founders is the national pitch competitions and roadshows, running across ten countries from October to November 2026. Nigeria is one of them. Each national round selects the startups that will represent their country at the Continental Pitch during the Congress itself.

The initiative is supported by Accelerate Africa, Villgro Africa, Africa Fintech Foundry, Startup Hub and the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP). Startups are assessed on innovation, scalability, traction and impact potential.

The reason gatherings like this matter is that they compress the distance between a founder and the people who can fund or unblock them. Capital, policy and founders end up in one room. For a Nigerian startup that is already raising, a warm introduction made in Kigali can be worth more than most competition cheques.
